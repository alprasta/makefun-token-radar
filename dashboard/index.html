Which pre-token protocol is worth farming next?

Every protocol below records no token, ranked by what its launch would be worth if its record stayed as it is. The watchlist is the floor, never the ceiling: the board also carries the early pre-token protocols below the $50M floor.

How the value is computed — model radar-2

implied value = TVL × category multiple × growth × maturity × chains

  • Category multiple. The market's own price for this kind of protocol, from the live-token protocols in the same category in this same snapshot. It is a shrinkage estimate, not a cutoff: a category with n live comparables is priced at (n / 5) × its own median market-cap / TVL + (1 − n / 5) × the overall live-token median, with n capped at 5. A category with 5 or more comparables is priced by its own median alone; a category with 1 to 4 keeps a real share of its own price instead of being thrown away; a category with none is priced at the market average, never at a guess. The multiple is clamped to 0.05–0.60. In this snapshot 26 of the board's 34 categories hold 1 to 4 live comparables — under the old 5-or-nothing rule they were all priced at one flat number, including the whole top of the board.
  • Growth. 1 + (7-day TVL change, clamped to −60%…+120%) / 100 × 0.5. No 7-day figure counts as 0%.
  • Maturity. 0.6 + min(age in days, 1095) / 1095 × 0.4. Age is the time from first listing to the snapshot date; an unknown listing date counts as 365 days. A protocol that has run longer with no token scores higher.
  • Chains. 1 + 0.10 × (1 − 1 / chains). One chain scores 1.00; many chains approach 1.10.
  • Farm plans. Every step of a protocol's six-step plan is derived from its own record, never copied from a template: the wallet step names its first chain and that chain's gas token, the weekly step is the recurring action of its own category (collect fees on a DEX, roll the hedge on a perpetual, restake rewards on a staking protocol), the hold step names its age and size, the claim step names where its kind of protocol pays out, and the log step names what its kind of position has to track.

One function scores every row from the snapshot's own figures — no protocol name is special-cased and no row is hand-picked. “Records no token” means DefiLlama's record carries no market cap, no CoinGecko id and no symbol; it is the record, not proof a token does not exist. Protocols whose token already trades are set aside in the Live view with their market caps, never mixed into the ranked board. Of the 758 protocols in the snapshot, 342 record no token; 144 of those are set aside by the ranking rules, in order — 128 in bridged, staked or exchange categories, 11 versioned pool names and 5 wrapped or staked products — leaving the 198 ranked below. The other 416 already carry a token in the record; the 24 largest by market cap are shown in the Live view. Implied value is this entry's own estimate, not a price and not a prediction that a token will launch.

Checks — the board proves its own arithmetic

Two checks run on the snapshot's own figures at build time, and the first runs again live in your browser below. They are robustness checks, not accuracy claims: the snapshot is one frozen point in time, so no backtest against later market caps can be run from it, and none is claimed here.

  • Every row recomputed. Each row's implied value is recomputed from the multipliers the row stores — the same arithmetic the detail view shows — and must match board.json exactly.
  • Order under wobble. The board is rebuilt four times with every protocol's TVL moved 10% up or down and its 7-day change moved 5 points either way. A board whose top flips on small moves in one figure is not a ranking; a board that keeps its top rows through the wobble is one.

The checks run from the same snapshot the board is built from, in the same program run, so they are deterministic: run the program twice and the numbers come out identical.
